I'm an M.S. student in Computer Science at the University of Washington's [Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering](https://www.cs.washington.edu/) and a graduate student researcher at the [Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)](https://allenai.org/). My research spans reinforcement learning for language models, multimodal foundation models, and robot learning.

At Ai2, I work on the AllenNLP scaling team, integrating multimodal capabilities into OLMo-core for mixture-of-experts language models. I'm also a member of the [Social RL Lab](https://socialrl.cs.washington.edu/), advised by [Prof. Natasha Jaques](https://natashajaques.ai/), where I study multi-turn and self-play reinforcement learning for LLMs, and I work with [Prof. Ranjay Krishna](https://ranjaykrishna.com/) in the [RAIVN Lab](https://raivn.cs.washington.edu/) and Ai2's PRIOR robotics team, where I was a core contributor to [MolmoAct2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.02881).

Before graduate school, I earned a B.S. in Computer Engineering with Distinction and a B.A. in Comparative History of Ideas from the University of Washington, where I wrote an honors thesis on hardware acceleration of neural networks with [Prof. Scott Hauck](https://people.ece.uw.edu/hauck/) in the [ACME Lab](https://people.ece.uw.edu/hauck/acme.html). I have also interned as a software engineer on AWS SageMaker at Amazon, and I've served as Head TA for UW's graduate and undergraduate machine learning courses.
